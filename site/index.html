One world, every scale

From a single cell to the cosmic web.

It's all one world and one camera. Nothing loads as you go, because every star and system grows from your world's seed on the spot. You can play at every step.

  1. Cell

    Dive into a tide pool and swim, bite and grow as a single cell.

  2. Creature

    Crawl ashore and walk the planet as your own species.

  3. Tribe

    Awaken a people. Gather, raid, dance and win villages over.

  4. City

    Lay roads, zones, power and water on the planet's own ground.

  5. Planet

    Sculpt the land, pour the seas, set the climate and the look.

  6. Star system

    Fly your own ship off the ground and on to the other worlds.

  7. Galaxy

    Nine thousand stars, rival empires, colony ships and space battles.

  8. Cosmic web

    Zoom past your galaxy and sculpt the web of galaxies itself.

Everything is clay

Real physics, and you can grab all of it.

Mountains, seas, moons, suns and whole galaxies all answer to your hand, and the world plays out what you did. The same goes for this page: and scatter these cards.

Sculpt and grab

Raise ranges, carve rivers, and grab a forest or an ice cap and pull it across the land.

Water that flows

Rain runs downhill into lakes, rivers and waterfalls, and the seas fill in, freeze or boil with the climate.

Weather and cataclysm

Seasons, storms, wildfire and cataclysms, all playing out on the ground you shaped.

Moons and worlds

Pull a moon down, smash two worlds together, or fling yours into deep space until a new sun catches it.

Clay cosmos

Grab, throw, grow and destroy suns and planets under real gravity.

Galaxies and the web

Scatter, gather and swirl whole galaxies. When they collide they merge, leaving tails and flung stars.

Design it, and the world uses it

The editors build the world.

What you design in Asterra's editors turns up in the game itself: your creatures, your buildings, your ships.

Creatures→species that evolve

Life on your world grows real bodies from the creature editor and keeps adapting them to its land.

Humans and aliens→citizens

The people editor dresses the crowds on your cities' sidewalks.

Buildings→cities in your style

Halls, homes, works and playhouses from the building editor become the cities your peoples raise.

Vehicles→fleets on land, sea and air

Armies, caravans and missionaries roll out in your designs, and you can take the wheel.

Starships and UFOs→the fleets in space

Empires fly your warships into battle across the galaxy.

Bases→outposts on any world

Floors, walls, habs, domes, storage, beacons and landing pads, placed part by part.

Built exact

One file. Same seed, same universe.

1file that runs offline. No install, no server.
97test suites every release has to pass
0loading screens from cell to cosmic web
2.3 MBfor the whole game

The simulation is deterministic. Each release is checked against the one before on eight golden scenarios, and a new feature can't quietly change how an old world plays.

Speed-ups have to produce exactly the same results as before, so when things get faster, your worlds still behave the same.
